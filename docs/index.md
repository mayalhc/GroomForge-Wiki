## The add-ons

### Hair

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   [![GroomForge PRO](assets/products/groomforge.jpg)](guide.md)

    ### GroomForge PRO

    Blender Hair Curves into an Unreal groom Unreal actually reads. Injects the
    attributes UE needs, builds hair cards with baked textures, rigs guide
    curves into bones, and sends the result straight to a running editor.

    ---

    Non-destructive grooming on top of Blender's native hair curves — braids,

-   [![MetaBridge DNA](assets/products/metabridge.jpg)](https://mayalhc.github.io/metabridge_dna/)

## How it fits together

![Blender to Unreal: grooming, hair flow and DNA sync](assets/products/ecosystem.jpg){ .cs-wide }

---

!!! question "Which one do I need?"

    **Styling the hair** → GroomFlow PRO.
    **Getting that hair into Unreal** → GroomForge PRO.
    **Making it move** → NovaBone Dynamics.
    **MetaHuman character data** → MetaBridge DNA in Blender, MetaBridge Forge in Unreal.
    **Animation** → MotionForge. **Effects** → VFXForge PRO.
