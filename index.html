    The add-ons¶

    Hair¶

    • GroomForge PRO¶

      Blender Hair Curves into an Unreal groom Unreal actually reads. Injects the attributes UE needs, builds hair cards with baked textures, rigs guide curves into bones, and sends the result straight to a running editor.

      Guide · 한국어 · Store

    • GroomFlow PRO¶

      Non-destructive grooming on top of Blender's native hair curves — braids, fur, cutting, hair ties, clump IDs and simulation you can freeze into the guides.

      Guide · 한국어 · Store

    Character¶

    • MetaBridge DNA¶

      MetaHuman DNA in and out of Blender: correctives, rig retargeting, wearables weight transfer, bake to .dna, and live link to Unreal and Cascadeur.

      Guide · 한국어 · Store

    • MetaBridge Forge¶

      An Unreal editor plugin. Pick one FBX or Alembic and it runs the whole MetaHuman outfit and groom preparation through to a verified Wardrobe Item, with no project content setup.

      Guide · Store

    Motion¶

    • NovaBone Dynamics¶

      Scale-independent bone-chain physics for hair, tails, skirts, accessories and jiggle bones. Pick a chain's first and last bone; it simulates, collides and bakes to keyframes.

      Guide · 한국어 · Store

    • MotionForge¶

      Motion generation and cleanup inside Blender, with a two-way live link to Cascadeur and retargeting onto your own rigs.

      Guide · 한국어 · Store

    • Wiggle 2: RTX Edition¶

      The layered bone physics engine, maintained for current Blender versions after the original was discontinued.

      Guide · News

    Effects¶

    • VFXForge PRO In development¶

      Particle and VDB effects built on Geometry Nodes, aimed at output a game engine can take. Not released yet — the guide is published as it is written.

      Guide

    How it fits together¶

    Which one do I need?

    Styling the hair → GroomFlow PRO. Getting that hair into Unreal → GroomForge PRO. Making it move → NovaBone Dynamics. MetaHuman character data → MetaBridge DNA in Blender, MetaBridge Forge in Unreal. Animation → MotionForge. Effects → VFXForge PRO.
